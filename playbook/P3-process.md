# Phase 3 — Process analysis — `/brd-p3-process`

**Follow:** `P0-phase-rules.md` · **Closes with:** APPROVED · **Output:** `brd/P3-process.md`

## Purpose
Describe the business process step by step as the system actually runs it today, and tie every step to the technology behind it.

## Produce
1. **Process overview:** the end-to-end process as a ```flow block.
2. **Step table:** for each step: what happens in business terms, who or what triggers it, the job / pipeline / procedure that performs it (OBJ ids), inputs and outputs, timing, and its label.
3. **Variants and exceptions:** month-end, corrections, reruns, late data, manual interventions found in documents, code or run history.
4. **Manual steps:** anything people do outside the system (from documents); mark clearly.
5. **Hand-offs:** where the process passes to or from another area.
6. **Questions** where documents and code disagree on order or behaviour (`### Q-…`, `### CLM-…`).

## Review page
The process picture, the step table, variants, manual steps, hand-offs, questions.
